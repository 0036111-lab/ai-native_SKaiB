# Customization and release checklist

## Replace before preview

- `[HERO_NAME]`, `[DISCIPLINE]`, `[GOAL]`, `[CITY]` and all placeholder copy.
- poster and video sources; do not ship the demo gradient as final media.
- timeline entries and evidence links.
- team names, roles and approved public contacts.
- form action and privacy link.
- title, description, canonical, Open Graph image and JSON-LD entities.

## Content QA

- Every number and title has a source and date.
- Age is calculated from a stored birth date or written with an “as of” date.
- Competition names and placements match official results.
- Future participation is ambition or probability, never an accomplished fact.
- Private invitations are paraphrased only with permission; financial terms stay
  behind the partner request.

## Experience QA

- No horizontal scrolling on 320–430 px screens.
- Headlines wrap without losing words.
- Video starts from the selected expressive moment and remains lightweight.
- Poster appears under slow network or reduced-data conditions.
- Menu, modal, close button, focus trap and Escape key work.
- Form success is shown only after a successful backend response.
- The page remains useful with JavaScript or animation disabled.

## SEO/GEO QA

- One descriptive H1; section headings follow a logical hierarchy.
- Named entity, discipline, location and current program appear naturally in the
  first screen and supporting copy.
- FAQ answers can stand alone when quoted by search or answer engines.
- Structured data contains only visible, verified facts.
- Official sources are linked near sensitive claims.
- Lighthouse targets: performance 85+, accessibility 95+, SEO 95+ on mobile.
