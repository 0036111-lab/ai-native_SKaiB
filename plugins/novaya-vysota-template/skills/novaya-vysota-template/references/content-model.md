# Content model

| Section | Visitor question | Required material | Primary motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Who is this and why now? | short video, identity, strongest proof, goal | video loop + title reveal |
| Proof | Can I believe it? | 4–6 sourced signals | ticker |
| Story | How did they get here? | origin, training arc, human detail | image/text stagger |
| Timeline | Is growth sustained? | dated results and transitions | progress line |
| Goal | What is at stake? | target, deadline, qualification context | large-number transition |
| Program | What happens next? | stages, current milestone, outputs | pinned cards |
| Validation | Who else believes in it? | public invitation or attributed quote | document/card reveal |
| Context | What is the deeper story? | family, culture or community archive | editorial gallery |
| Platform | Who makes it work? | philosophy, model, future directions | split-grid reveal |
| Partners | Why participate? | value, formats, reporting | benefit rows |
| Team | Who is accountable? | roles, public contacts | simple cards |
| CTA | What do I do? | one action, form, fallback contact | modal or inline form |

## Two-contour narrative

The hero contour supplies emotion and evidence. The platform contour supplies
scale and trust. Do not alternate them from the first screen; reveal them in that
order, then unite them in the partner offer.

## Evidence pattern

For each public claim store: wording, status, source URL/file, source date,
reviewer and last-checked date. Unverified claims may guide design but must not
appear as facts in production.
